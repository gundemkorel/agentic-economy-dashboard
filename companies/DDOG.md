# DDOG — Datadog

## Research role

Benchmark, not a current thesis candidate or valuation call.

## Why it is useful

Datadog offers a high-quality comparison point for whether AI-enabled observability, security, and increasingly autonomous operations can be carried by a software model with strong operating leverage and cash returns.

## Reported baseline — Q2 2026

- Revenue: $1.12B, +36% year over year.
- Non-GAAP operating income: $257M, 23% margin.
- Operating cash flow: $316M.
- Free cash flow: $279M, approximately 25% margin.

## Boundary

AI product activity, autonomous-remediation functionality, and aggregate growth are not agent-specific revenue disclosure. The company remains a benchmark until the project writes a named billable mechanism, repeated commercial proof, and point-in-time expectations history.
