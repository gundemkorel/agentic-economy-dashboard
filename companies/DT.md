# DT — Dynatrace

## Dated gap review — September 24, 2026

**Research state: monitor / fiscal alignment and acquisition execution required.** Dynatrace has credible operating signals for AI-observability demand, including sharply rising log consumption and an expanded agent product suite. The proposed Arize acquisition increases strategic relevance but introduces acquisition and margin execution risk. The live market snapshot is not aligned to Dynatrace's March fiscal year.

This is a research conclusion, not an investment recommendation.

## Wave-2 mechanism

More autonomous software can generate more traces, logs, security and performance events, and a stronger need to evaluate, operate, and govern AI applications. Dynatrace captures the thesis only if that activity produces incremental ARR, consumption, retention, and cash flow.

## What is observed

- **Core demand is strong:** Q1 FY2027 total ARR was $2.136 billion, up 17% year over year; total revenue was $554.5 million, up 16%; and subscription revenue was $530.3 million, up 16%.
- **The relevant consumption proxy is accelerating:** Annualized logs consumption reached $200 million, growing more than 100% year over year.
- **Contracted base is sizable:** Remaining performance obligations were $3.442 billion, with 53% expected to be recognized over the next twelve months.
- **Agent-product fit is explicit:** Dynatrace released Autonomous SRE agent, Cloud SRE agent, and Agent Building capabilities, and put an observability offering for AI-first teams into private preview.
- **Arize is strategically material but not yet realized:** Dynatrace announced a proposed $915 million acquisition of AI-observability specialist Arize. Management expects it to add roughly 200 basis points to FY2027 ARR growth and dilute FY2027 non-GAAP operating margin by roughly 175 basis points; these are forward-looking expectations, not reported results.

## Why the capture case is not proven yet

- Dynatrace does not disclose AI-observability or agent-specific ARR, revenue, customer count, usage, pricing, or retention.
- Subscription gross margin fell to 85% from 86%, which the 10-Q attributes primarily to higher cloud-hosting costs driven by SaaS utilization and platform-feature adoption.
- Rising logs consumption can reflect ordinary cloud modernization as well as agentic activity. It is a relevant proxy, not attribution.
- The Arize deal remains subject to closing and integration. It is expected to dilute non-GAAP operating margin before any longer-term synergy is realized.
- The current Eulerpool snapshot has a December 31 fiscal date, while Dynatrace FY2027 ends March 31, 2027. It must not be compared with the FY2027 guide.

## Expectation-gap discipline

The live [Expectations Gap dashboard](https://gundemkorel.github.io/agentic-economy-dashboard/expectations.html) now flags fiscal-year mismatch automatically. The right current interpretation is **not comparable** rather than a conclusion about valuation or revisions.

If a fiscal-year-aligned consensus becomes available, the important test is whether logs / AI-observability demand and the Arize integration produce ARR acceleration that outweighs margin pressure faster than consensus revises.

## What would upgrade the thesis

1. Sustained acceleration in organic net new ARR, logs consumption, and subscription revenue with explicit AI-observability attribution.
2. Evidence that Autonomous SRE, Agent Building, Bluebox, or Arize convert into paid usage, retention, or cross-sell.
3. Successful Arize close and integration with actual ARR contribution at or above management's stated outlook.
4. Stable or improving subscription gross margin and free-cash-flow trajectory despite cloud-hosting costs and acquisition dilution.

## Thesis breakers

1. Logs consumption grows without an incremental revenue, pricing, or retention benefit.
2. SaaS hosting costs and acquisition dilution outweigh the value of AI-observability growth.
3. Arize integration misses expectations, delays the product roadmap, or dilutes margins for longer than expected.
4. Enterprises choose hyperscaler-native, open-source, or other observability platforms for AI workloads.

## Quarterly checklist

- Total ARR, organic net new ARR, subscription revenue, logs consumption, and RPO.
- AI-observability / agent product adoption, customer usage, pricing, and any explicit revenue disclosure.
- Arize transaction status, ARR contribution, integration costs, and margin impact.
- Subscription gross margin, non-GAAP operating margin, and adjusted free cash flow.
- A period-aligned consensus series before assessing an expectation gap.
