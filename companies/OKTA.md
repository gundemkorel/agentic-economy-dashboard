# OKTA — Okta

## Research role

Secondary candidate. First reported baseline collected on August 26, 2026; no investment or expectation-gap conclusion.

## Wave-2 mechanism

Autonomous agents that act for users or systems require identity, delegated authority, access policy, and auditability. Okta could capture that requirement only if agent identity becomes an incremental paid workload rather than a bundled platform capability.

## Economics that must work

- **Billable unit:** subscription identity platform and any paid agent-identity extension.
- **Agentic trigger:** more delegated agents increase identity, authorization, policy, and audit demands.
- **Proof threshold:** disclosed agent-identity customer adoption, pricing, attach, transactions, revenue, or retention with durable RPO, margin, and cash evidence.
- **Time to impact:** 12–36 months.

## Reported baseline — Q2 FY2027

- Revenue: $805M, +11% year over year; subscription revenue: $793M, +12%.
- RPO: $4.858B, +17%; cRPO: $2.585B, +14%.
- Non-GAAP operating income: $226M, 28% margin.
- Free cash flow: $227M, 28% of revenue.

## What would advance the research

1. Agent identity becomes paid and measurable rather than an architecture or ecosystem integration.
2. Subscription, RPO, and cRPO growth demonstrate durable demand after any reporting-comparability changes.
3. Margins and cash returns remain intact while new workload investments scale.

## What can break the mechanism

- Agent identity is free, bundled, partner-led, or non-material.
- Broad identity demand slows while agent relevance stays unquantified.
- Service-partner changes or investments obscure the underlying trend.
