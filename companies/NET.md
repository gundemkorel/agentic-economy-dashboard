# NET — Cloudflare

## Research role

Benchmark, not a current thesis candidate or valuation call.

## Why it is useful

Cloudflare is a broad reference point for the convergence of edge delivery, developer infrastructure, AI controls, identity, and emerging agent-payment rails. It helps frame the competitive ceiling faced by more focused candidates.

## Reported baseline — Q2 2026

- Revenue: $696.1M, +36% year over year.
- Current RPO: +35% year over year.
- Non-GAAP operating margin: 13.8%.
- Free cash flow: $56.4M, 8% of revenue.

## Boundary

Relevant products and rapid growth do not prove agent-specific revenue, nor do they reveal what the market expects. The company remains a comparison point until it is researched through the same causal, commercial, and expectation-alignment gates as a candidate.
