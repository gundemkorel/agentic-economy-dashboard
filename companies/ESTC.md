# ESTC — Elastic

## Dated gap review — September 24, 2026

**Research state: monitor / fiscal alignment required.** Elastic has one of the more direct economic paths from agentic activity to consumption: search, retrieval, vector workloads, and observability can increase Elastic Cloud usage. The company does not disclose how much of that growth is agent-driven, and the current market snapshot cannot yet be compared with its April fiscal year guidance.

This is a research conclusion, not an investment recommendation.

## Wave-2 mechanism

Agentic applications require retrieval, embeddings, vector search, observability, investigation, and security workflows. Elastic can benefit when these workloads consume Elastic Cloud capacity or expand paid subscriptions; it does not benefit merely because the products exist.

## What is observed

- **Cloud exposure is meaningful:** Q1 FY2027 Elastic Cloud revenue was $235.2 million, up 20% year over year and 49% of total revenue. The company says the vast majority of Elastic Cloud subscriptions are consumption-based.
- **The base business is growing:** Total revenue was $478 million, up 15%; sales-led subscription revenue was $399 million, up 18%; cRPO was $1.153 billion, up 21%; and total RPO was $1.854 billion, up 27%.
- **Expansion remains healthy:** Net expansion rate was about 111%, and the number of customers with more than $100,000 of ACV exceeded 1,800.
- **Product fit is real:** Elastic disclosed agentic Kubernetes investigations, MCP-based observability skills, agentic-SOC workflows, VectorDB index mode, and an AI-powered investigation acquisition. These are relevance signals, not a separately disclosed revenue category.

## Why the capture case is not proven yet

- Elastic does not report revenue, usage, customer count, pricing, retention, or margin specifically attributable to agents, vector workloads, or MCP workflows.
- Total gross margin fell to 74% from 77%, and subscription gross margin fell to 80% from 82%. The 10-Q attributes the pressure largely to cloud hosting costs rising faster than subscription revenue.
- RPO excludes on-demand arrangements without minimum purchase commitments. That means the most consumption-sensitive portion of Elastic Cloud is not fully represented by contracted RPO.
- The current Eulerpool snapshot is labeled for a December 31 fiscal year, whereas Elastic's FY2027 ends April 30, 2027. It is therefore not a valid guide-versus-consensus comparison.

## Expectation-gap discipline

The live [Gap Readiness dashboard](https://gundemkorel.github.io/agentic-economy-dashboard/expectations.html) shows the provider's current fiscal date beside the company outlook. Until a consensus series is aligned to Elastic's April year-end and GAAP / non-GAAP definitions, the right conclusion is **not comparable**, not cheap or expensive.

Even after alignment, the bar for an upside gap is higher than “Elastic is involved in AI.” We would need to see incremental consumption, growth, or retention that is visibly tied to agentic workloads and exceeds what the aligned consensus already assumes.

## What would upgrade the thesis

1. Elastic Cloud growth, consumption, and annual-cloud mix continue to outpace the broader business without a worsening margin profile.
2. Management discloses agentic search, vector, AI-security, or observability usage / revenue / customer adoption metrics.
3. cRPO, RPO, and net expansion remain strong alongside greater agentic product adoption.
4. A fiscal-year-aligned consensus source makes it possible to measure whether revisions lag operating evidence.

## Thesis breakers

1. Agentic workloads use Elastic features but do not create incremental paid consumption, retention, or pricing.
2. Cloud-hosting costs keep rising faster than the revenue they enable, compressing gross margin.
3. Usage growth is driven by conventional search, logging, or security workloads rather than the proposed Wave-2 mechanism.
4. Larger cloud or AI-platform vendors capture the economic value of vector, retrieval, and observability workloads.

## Quarterly checklist

- Elastic Cloud revenue, annual versus monthly mix, consumption commentary, and cloud-hosting cost.
- Sales-led subscription revenue, cRPO, total RPO, net expansion, and large-customer additions.
- Any agent-specific customer, usage, pricing, or revenue disclosure.
- Subscription and total gross margin, non-GAAP operating margin, and adjusted free-cash-flow margin.
- A live consensus snapshot only after fiscal year and metric definitions are aligned.
