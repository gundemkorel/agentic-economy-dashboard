# TWLO — Twilio

## Research role

Secondary candidate. First reported baseline collected on August 6, 2026; no investment or expectation-gap conclusion.

## Wave-2 mechanism

Agents that communicate with customers, suppliers, or internal teams can multiply paid messaging, voice, verification, and engagement events. The important question is whether those events bring incremental gross profit and durable customer expansion.

## Focused proof review — September 25, 2026

Twilio's 2026 proxy reports **49% year-over-year growth in FY2025 Voice AI customer revenue**, but gives no absolute revenue base or ConversationRelay-only contribution. Its Q2 2026 investor deck names multiple AI-related customer wins, including seven-figure *multi-product* agreements with Eltropy and Lirio and a seven-figure Twilio Conversations-layer deal with Car Finance 247. These are stronger commercial signals than a product launch, but the contract amounts cannot be assigned solely to Relay or to incremental agent-driven revenue.

The vendor-hosted Scorpion case reports 6,500 appointments it says would have been missed, a 39% higher average booking rate, and $8.4 million of Scorpion Convert revenue in its first three months. It is not a matched-control study and **customer revenue is not Twilio revenue or profit**. Relay is billed per active AI-agent session minute, with other communications charges potentially separate. The remaining bridge is paid Relay/Voice minutes × realized net price − speech, carrier, support, and displacement cost. No reviewed source supplies that cohort-level bridge yet.

Twilio also raised FY2026 company-issued FCF guidance to $1.135–$1.155 billion in its Q2 release. That is a company outlook for the whole business, not Street consensus or agent-attributable FCF. The active research test is whether comparable agent-exposed customers generate more *net-new completed jobs* and more Twilio gross profit after costs than non-agent controls. Keep the [Economic Map](https://gundemkorel.github.io/agentic-economy-dashboard/economic-map.html) hurdle illustrative until those inputs exist.

## Economics that must work

- **Billable unit:** communications and engagement events, with software and platform layers.
- **Agentic trigger:** autonomous outreach, follow-up, verification, and service workflows create more paid interactions.
- **Proof threshold:** agent-related volume or paid adoption tied to revenue, pricing, retention, or gross-margin outcomes.
- **Time to impact:** 6–24 months.

## Reported baseline — Q2 2026

- Revenue: $1.499B, +22% reported and +17% organic year over year.
- Dollar-based net expansion: 116%.
- Non-GAAP gross profit: $736M, 49% margin.
- Free cash flow: $353M, 24% of revenue.

## What would advance the research

1. The company discloses paid agent-driven activity, customer adoption, revenue, price, or retention evidence.
2. Organic growth and customer expansion stay durable across comparable cohorts.
3. Carrier costs and mix do not consume the incremental economics of higher event volume.

## What can break the mechanism

- Agent functionality is bundled or creates low-margin traffic.
- Carrier costs compress gross margin as communications volume scales.
- Broad platform growth continues without a disclosed agentic link.
