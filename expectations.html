When a gap call becomes valid

All required
A

Comparable operating evidence

At least two dated company observations that show a causal KPI improving, with the counterargument tracked alongside it.

B

Aligned expectations history

Fiscal-period-aligned consensus, convention labels, and at least two point-in-time estimate observations to establish revision direction.

C

Valuation history

A contemporaneous multiple or cash-flow valuation history. Price targets remain supplemental context, never the proof of a gap.
