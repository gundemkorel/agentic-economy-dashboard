# ZS — Zscaler

## Research role

Secondary candidate. First reported baseline collected on September 3, 2026; no investment or expectation-gap conclusion.

## Wave-2 mechanism

Enterprise agents expand the number of identities, applications, data paths, and tools that need policy enforcement. Zscaler could capture that activity through paid zero-trust, data-security, and security-for-agents controls.

## Economics that must work

- **Billable unit:** platform subscriptions and non-seat-based security spend.
- **Agentic trigger:** more autonomous access to applications, data, and tools raises the need for policy, data protection, and agent security.
- **Proof threshold:** disclosed paid agent-security adoption, attach, price, transaction volume, revenue, or retention, alongside comparable organic ARR and adequate cash conversion.
- **Time to impact:** 12–36 months.

## Reported baseline — Q4 FY2026

- Revenue: $898.2M, +25% year over year.
- ARR: $3.771B, +25%; excluding Red Canary, $3.630B, +20%.
- Non-GAAP operating margin: 24%.
- Free cash flow: $60.8M, 7% of revenue, after $218.5M in capex and internal-use software.

## What would advance the research

1. Security-for-agents becomes commercially measurable, rather than a product description.
2. Organic ARR and net-new ARR remain strong after separating acquisition effects.
3. Capacity investment produces durable growth without an uncontained cash-return penalty.

## What can break the mechanism

- Agent security is bundled into existing contracts or becomes a generic platform requirement.
- Red Canary or pricing changes obscure organic demand.
- Capital investment stays high while free-cash-flow conversion does not recover.
