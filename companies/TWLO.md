# TWLO — Twilio

## Research role

Secondary candidate. First reported baseline collected on August 6, 2026; no investment or expectation-gap conclusion.

## Wave-2 mechanism

Agents that communicate with customers, suppliers, or internal teams can multiply paid messaging, voice, verification, and engagement events. The important question is whether those events bring incremental gross profit and durable customer expansion.

## Economics that must work

- **Billable unit:** communications and engagement events, with software and platform layers.
- **Agentic trigger:** autonomous outreach, follow-up, verification, and service workflows create more paid interactions.
- **Proof threshold:** agent-related volume or paid adoption tied to revenue, pricing, retention, or gross-margin outcomes.
- **Time to impact:** 6–24 months.

## Reported baseline — Q2 2026

- Revenue: $1.499B, +22% reported and +17% organic year over year.
- Dollar-based net expansion: 116%.
- Non-GAAP gross profit: $736M, 49% margin.
- Free cash flow: $353M, 24% of revenue.

## What would advance the research

1. The company discloses paid agent-driven activity, customer adoption, revenue, price, or retention evidence.
2. Organic growth and customer expansion stay durable across comparable cohorts.
3. Carrier costs and mix do not consume the incremental economics of higher event volume.

## What can break the mechanism

- Agent functionality is bundled or creates low-margin traffic.
- Carrier costs compress gross margin as communications volume scales.
- Broad platform growth continues without a disclosed agentic link.
