# FSLY — Fastly

## Research role

Secondary candidate. First reported baseline collected on August 5, 2026; no investment or expectation-gap conclusion.

## Wave-2 mechanism

Machine retrieval, inference-adjacent requests, and AI application delivery can add demand for edge delivery, security, compute, and observability. Traffic only matters economically when it turns into recurring spend with healthy cash conversion.

## Economics that must work

- **Billable unit:** edge delivery, security, compute, and observability consumption.
- **Agentic trigger:** more machine traffic and AI applications increase request, security, and runtime-control needs.
- **Proof threshold:** paid AI-delivery, AI-firewall, compute, security, or observability adoption linked to revenue and durable retention.
- **Time to impact:** 6–24 months.

## Reported baseline — Q2 2026

- Revenue: $183.3M, +23% year over year.
- Security revenue: $41.7M, +43% year over year.
- RPO: $341M, +38%; LTM net retention: 117%.
- Operating cash flow: $39.3M; free cash flow: $3.6M.

## What would advance the research

1. The company connects AI or machine traffic to paid adoption, usage, pricing, or revenue.
2. RPO and retention stay strong without worsening customer concentration.
3. Gross margin and free cash flow become more convincing as the network scales.

## What can break the mechanism

- Machine traffic rises but is not monetized through a differentiated paid product.
- Customer concentration or low-quality usage drives the result.
- Network and operating costs prevent meaningful cash conversion.
