Audit trail / research process

Evidence, not
an index of vibes.

Observed data, company reporting, consensus estimates, and our own scenarios stay structurally separate. Sparse and honest beats synthetic-looking precision.

A

Triangulate

Cloudflare, Fastly, Akamai, and Zscaler each see a different slice of activity. Investigate disagreement; never average it away.

B

Track capture separately

Wave-2 acceleration does not automatically translate into a particular company’s revenue or free cash flow.

C

Preserve provenance

Every observation needs source, period, retrieval date, definition, methodology, and an observed/estimated label.
