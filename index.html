Read me

Methodology & update rhythm

A

Separate signal from capture

Layers 01–02 measure whether machine activity and enterprise control are changing. Layer 03 measures whether named companies monetize that change.

B

Prefer observable proxies

Use public telemetry, product disclosures, usage reports, earnings commentary, and filings. Record the source, period, direction, and confidence for every metric.

C

Do not composite too early

Keep the component measures separate until enough history exists to test whether a composite index adds information instead of fake precision.
