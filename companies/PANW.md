# PANW — Palo Alto Networks

## Research role

Benchmark, not a current thesis candidate or valuation call.

## Why it is useful

Palo Alto Networks provides a large-scale comparison point for how a broad security platform might absorb agent-control workloads. It also highlights the risk that platform consolidation can limit standalone monetization for smaller security and control-plane candidates.

## Reported baseline — Q4 FY2026

- Revenue: $3.41B, +34% year over year.
- Next-Generation Security ARR: $9.10B, +63% year over year.
- RPO: $21.2B, +34% year over year.
- Full-year adjusted free-cash-flow margin: 38.4%.

## Boundary

Aggregate platform metrics cannot be attributed to agentic workloads. Acquisitions, integration, and a broad product base further complicate causal interpretation. The company remains a comparison point until agent-specific commercial evidence and expectations history are independently examined.
